📜 TELEGRAPH FAQ

📡 How do I enter multi-tap letters like S (• • •)?

Tap three times quickly. The game gives you 1 second gap after your last tap to finish building the letter pattern before committing it!

⌨️ Can I use keyboard controls?

Yes, hold and release the Spacebar just like the telegraph key.
