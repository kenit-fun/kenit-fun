❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Triple Threat? +
Triple Threat is a word puzzle game where one single English word fits into three different blanks in a sentence. It tests your vocabulary and mastery over homonyms (words with multiple meanings).
How does scoring work? +
Each correct guess gives you +10 points plus a streak bonus! However, using hints deducts points: Letter Clue (-2 pts), Meaning Clue (-3 pts), and Reveal Word (-15 pts).
Are there more puzzles available? +
Yes, this game contains 50 handcrafted puzzles that are shuffled randomly every time you start or restart a session!
