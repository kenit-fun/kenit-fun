📜 TYPEWRITER FAQ

⌨️ How do I type on the paper?

Click or tap anywhere inside the cream-colored paper deck and start typing on your keyboard to produce mechanical sounds and vintage typewriter characters.

🎨 How do I switch ink ribbon color?

Click any color spool at the top menu to instantly switch between Carbon Black, Crimson Red, Navy Blue, Forest Green, and Royal Purple ink.

🔔 What is that bell sound on Enter?

The bell signifies a carriage return, mimicking the mechanical margin bell that warned writers they reached the end of a line.
