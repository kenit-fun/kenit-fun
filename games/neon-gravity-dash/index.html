❓ HOW TO PLAY

1. 🎯 HOOK GRAVITY NODES

Tap and Hold anywhere on the screen when your orb is near a blue glowing gravity node to lock a tether beam.

2. 🔄 ORBIT & GAIN SPEED

Holding down makes your orb swing around the node in a high-speed orbital arc.

3. 🚀 RELEASE TO SLINGSHOT

Release your finger/mouse at the right angle to launch forward using centrifugal momentum.

4. ⚠️ DODGE RED HAZARDS

Avoid touching red plasma spheres or falling off screen limits!
