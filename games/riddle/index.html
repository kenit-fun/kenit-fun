How do I solve riddles in the vault? +
Read each riddle carefully and type your best guess in the box. Answers are not case-sensitive and accept common keywords associated with the solution.
What if my answer is correct but not accepted? +
Try typing just the core noun or singular form of the word (e.g. use "shadow" instead of "a dark shadow").
