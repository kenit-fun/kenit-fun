❓ How does the mechanical dial work?

Click any numbered hole. The wheel rotates down to the metal stop plate on the bottom right, then smoothly winds back into position while generating vintage click sounds.

🕹️ Hidden Easter Egg numbers

Try dialing: 555-0199 (KenitFun Line), 867-5309 (Jenny), 911-0000 (Operator), or 000-0000 (Dead Line).

📞 How to clear dialed digits?

Click the HANG UP / RESET bar at the top of the phone body to clear your screen instantly.
