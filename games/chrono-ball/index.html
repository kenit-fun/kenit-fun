🌀 How does time-dilation work?

Time moves in direct proportion to the core's movement speed. When the ball stops, time freezes, giving you complete safety to calculate your next slingshot trajectory.

🔮 What are Sinks and Crystals?

Crystals (Glowing Gold): Collect to gain points and replenish time energy.
Gravity Sinks (Swirling Black Holes): Avoid them! They pull your ball off-course and siphon energy quickly.
