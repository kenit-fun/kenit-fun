A three-ending station story

Midnight Station is a short branching story set on a foggy platform. Read the scene and choose one of the buttons to decide what your character does next. Each choice reveals another part of the mystery, and your route can end in one of three different ways.

  1. Begin on the platform and choose whether to answer the ringing phone or inspect the ticket booth.
  2. Read each new scene before choosing your next action; the text contains clues for the branch you're following.
  3. When an ending appears, select Play Again to return to the opening and try a different route.

There is no timer or score to beat. The experience is designed for exploration, so try each first choice and compare how the ending changes.
