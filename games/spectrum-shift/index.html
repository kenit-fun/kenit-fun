❓ HOW TO PLAY

1. 🎨 COLOR SHIFTING

Tap Cyan, Magenta, or Gold buttons at the bottom to shift your Orb's color instantly.

2. ⚡ PHASE THROUGH LASERS

Your Orb can ONLY pass through laser gates that match its CURRENT color.

3. 💥 COMBO MULTIPLIER

Pass through consecutive gates without mistakes to trigger Combo Score Multipliers!

4. 🚫 COLOR MISMATCH

Hitting a laser gate with the wrong color causes instant fragmentation!
