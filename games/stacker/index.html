🪨 ZEN BALANCER FAQ

🎯 How do I play?

Drag stones from the left and right sides and carefully place them on the center stone pedestal. Stack all 5 stones without knocking them over.

⚖️ How does the physics engine work?

Every stone features real-time gravity, friction, and angular velocity. Placing a stone off-center exerts rotational force on lower stones, triggering realistic collapses.

🏆 How do I trigger Zen Harmony?

Align all 5 stones in order of size directly above the pedestal and release your touch. Keep the tower motionless for a split second to clear the level.
