🪨 ZEN BALANCER FAQ

🎯 How do I play?

Drag stones from both sides using your touch screen or mouse and carefully place them on the pedestal. Stack all 5 stones safely.

⚖️ How does physics work?

Every stone features real-time gravity, friction, and momentum. Placing off-center causes realistic domino collapses.

🏆 How do I win?

Align all 5 stones vertically above the pedestal and release. Keep the tower stable for a split second to clear.
