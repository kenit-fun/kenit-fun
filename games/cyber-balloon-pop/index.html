CYBER BALLOON POP

Tap or slice floating neon balloons before they escape! Watch out for bombs!

❓ HOW TO PLAY

1. 🎈 TAP OR SLICE TO POP

Tap balloons individually or swipe/drag your finger across multiple balloons to pop them in a combo!

2. 🟡 SPECIAL GOLD ORBS

Golden balloons grant +30 bonus points!

3. ⏱️ FREEZE ORBS

Popping blue freeze orbs slows down time for 4 seconds.

4. 💣 AVOID RED BOMBS

Do NOT pop red spiked bombs or you will lose 1 life!
