    📜 SAFE CRACKER FAQ

    🔐 How do I play?

    Guess the secret 3-digit code (between 100 and 999). You have 7 attempts to unlock the vault.

    🎯 How do hints work?

    After each attempt, the system will tell you if your guess is TOO HIGH or TOO LOW. If you get within 15 units, it warns you: 🔥 Getting Warmer!

    🔄 Can I play again with a new code?

    Yes! Click the New Code button at any time to generate a brand-new secret combination and reset your attempts.
