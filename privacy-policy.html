Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information may be processed when you browse KenitFun or use one of its games. KenitFun does not require an account to play. Some optional features connect to third-party services, as described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to register or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include in your message. Our hosting provider and third-party services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, device, and request time when they deliver the site or a feature.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Storage use depends on the game:

  • Local storage: Some games save scores, settings, or an hourly-vote flag in your browser. This information stays in that browser and is not tied to a KenitFun account. Clearing this site's browser storage removes it.
  • Analytics: The home page loads Google Analytics to measure site visits. Google may process technical or usage information under its own policies.
  • Cookies: Google advertising or analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies. Their use is governed by Google's policies and settings.

3. Third-Party Advertising

KenitFun loads the Google AdSense script on site pages to support advertising. If ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads depending on your settings, location, and how Google serves ads. Learn more in Google's advertising information and manage personalization in My Ad Center.

4. Global Telegraph Messages

If you submit a message in Global Telegraph, the message text and a country label are sent to a shared public channel using HiveMQ's MQTT service. Other people connected to that channel may receive and read the message. The game requests an approximate country using your IP address through ipapi.co; the country label is included with the message. Do not submit your name, contact details, precise location, or other private or sensitive information. HiveMQ and ipapi.co are independent services and may process network information under their own policies.

5. Other External Services

Some games connect to external services to provide their features, including a shared MQTT message channel and an informal hourly poll service. Links, fonts, libraries, analytics, hosting, and advertising may also be provided by third parties. Those providers have their own privacy practices; review their policies before using a connected feature.

6. Your Choices

You can choose not to use a game that connects to an external service, clear KenitFun's local storage in your browser, or adjust cookie and advertising settings provided by your browser and Google. If you have a privacy question or want to contact us about information you sent directly to KenitFun, email us using the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

KenitFun does not require children to create accounts or provide personal details to play. The public message feature is visible to other users, so it should not be used to share personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can review it.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at kenitfun@gmail.com.
